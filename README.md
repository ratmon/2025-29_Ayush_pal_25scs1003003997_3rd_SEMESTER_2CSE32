📌 About the Internship

The internship was undertaken as a Web Developer Intern at Codec Technologies Pvt. Ltd. The official offer letter describes Codec Technologies as a global platform focused on empowering learners, connecting diverse talent with career opportunities, and delivering IT and business consultancy services across 27+ countries.

The internship provided practical exposure to an industry-oriented development environment. The work was structured around assigned projects and training tasks, with activities communicated through the organization's platform and email.

The internship helped connect academic knowledge from the B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering programme with practical concepts such as development workflow, problem solving, testing, debugging, review, and professional communication.

🔄 Development Workflow

The internship report follows a practical development workflow:

Requirement → Planning → Development → Testing → Review → Improvement → Submission

Workflow Stages

Requirement — Understand the assigned task and expected output.

Planning — Break the task into smaller and manageable steps.

Development — Implement the planned solution.

Testing — Check functionality and identify issues.

Review — Compare the work with requirements and available feedback.

Improvement — Correct issues and refine the result.

Submission — Complete and communicate the assigned work through the specified internship channels.
